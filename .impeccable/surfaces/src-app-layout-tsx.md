## Scope

Whole app shell and every tab: Home, Days, Bookings, Ideas, To-dos, Settings, plus sheets, photo viewer and form controls. Mode: Operate. Phone PWA and Mac desktop are equal citizens. Logic, data, server actions and handlers are untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The trip is a print series. Every day is a numbered station; every night takes the colour of where you sleep; an unprinted night is the alarm. Refuses the category default of white cards, one blue accent and an itinerary list, and refuses the old iOS grouped-list skin with its vermilion glow.

OWN-WORLD: Prussian blue (bero-ai) owns the shell: a solid sky header that fades by bokashi into a cool fog ground, and a Prussian desktop rail. White sheets with hairline edges sit on the fog; in dark mode the ground is night Prussian. City colours are flat fields. Vermilion is reserved for empty nights and overdue items, ochre for due-soon, pine for done. SF Pro for everything Latin with tabular numerals; Japanese names in mincho. Station cartouches: a small double-ruled rectangle holding a day's number.

STORY: Open the app and see how many days remain and which nights are unprinted; tap one to fill it. On Days, scan the series station by station. During the trip, Home leads with today's station, the next move and tonight's bed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone Home: Prussian sky header holding eyebrow, trip title and countdown in large tabular numerals; below on the fog, the route as a strip of station tiles tinted by city with hatched vermilion gaps; then empty nights as rows led by a cartouche; tab bar with Prussian active state. Desktop: Prussian rail at left, the same sky across the content column, two-column work area.

FORM: Tōkaidō Stations, candidate 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:7bd7b4159b64c1a6. Signature move: the station cartouche plus bokashi sky band, used only for day identity, the header sky and the route strip.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
